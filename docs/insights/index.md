# Insights

**Insight pages** are cross-domain syntheses that combine several standards and
frameworks around a single practical reporting question. Rather than describing
one standard in isolation, each insight traces how a theme — climate disclosure,
materiality, the SME pathway — is treated across the regimes covered by this
knowledge base.

These pages are composed from the current corpus and are **regenerated** whenever
new sources are ingested (they are declared in `config/synthesis.yaml`). They
carry a "Generated page" banner and should not be hand-edited — to change one,
edit its source domain pages or the synthesis configuration.
