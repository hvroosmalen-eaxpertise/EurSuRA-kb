# Climate Change Resilience Terminology

Climate change resilience terminology refers to the evolving lexicon used to describe concepts, strategies, and practices aimed at enhancing the ability of systems—social, ecological, and economic—to withstand, adapt to, and recover from the impacts of climate change. This terminology is shaped by academic research, policy development, and community engagement, and it reflects the complexity and interdisciplinary nature of climate change resilience.

The terminology for climate change resilience is rapidly expanding due to the growth of scientific literature and the increasing need for precise definitions to support effective policy and management responses. Terms related to climate change resilience often originate from various disciplines, including environmental science, conservation biology, and policy studies. These terms are used to describe a wide range of phenomena, from physical and ecological responses to social and economic strategies for adaptation and mitigation. The ambiguity and frequent misunderstandings of these terms have led to calls for more precise formulations and clarification, particularly in the context of cross-jurisdictional adaptation efforts and adaptive management frameworks.

The terminology is also influenced by the need for clear and unambiguous definitions to facilitate communication and ensure consistent application across different disciplines and spatial and temporal scales. This is particularly important in the context of conservation efforts, where the meanings of terms can significantly affect the design and implementation of management strategies. The development of a comprehensive glossary of terms related to climate change resilience is therefore essential for advancing understanding and improving the effectiveness of climate change adaptation and resilience initiatives.
