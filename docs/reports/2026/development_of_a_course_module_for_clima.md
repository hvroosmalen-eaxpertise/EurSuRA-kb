# Climate Governance and ETF Compliance MRV Systems for Sustainable Development

The course "Climate Governance and ETF Compliance MRV Systems for Sustainable Development" is designed for undergraduate students in their third year, offering three credits and a total duration of 45 hours (3 hours per week for 15 weeks). The course introduces students to the Enhanced Transparency Framework (ETF) under the Paris Agreement and its application to Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) systems, with a focus on the Agriculture, Forestry, and Other Land Use (AFOLU) sector in Sri Lanka, which contributes 25.1% (4.71 million tonnes CO2e) of the country's total greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions according to the C-NetZero 2050 report [^1]. Students will gain practical knowledge on designing, implementing, and managing MRV systems to meet ETF compliance requirements and monitor Sri Lanka’s climate commitments effectively [^1].

## Background

The course is structured to provide a comprehensive understanding of global climate governance frameworks, including the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC), the Kyoto Protocol (1997), the Paris Agreement (2015), and the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. It also explores the role of Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) in shaping national climate policies and the importance of climate finance and capacity building for developing countries [^1]. The course emphasizes the Enhanced Transparency Framework (ETF) and its role in ensuring transparency and accountability in climate actions, as well as the importance of MRV systems in achieving these goals [^1].

## Course Modules and Content Overview

### Module 1: Introduction to Climate Governance and the Paris Agreement

This module provides an overview of climate change governance and its implications for countries like Sri Lanka. It covers key global climate agreements, including the UNFCCC, Kyoto Protocol, Paris Agreement, Convention on Biological Diversity, 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, and the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction. The module also explores key climate action goals, such as limiting global warming to well below 2°C, achieving net-zero emissions by 2050, and promoting carbon markets and international cooperation [^1].

### Module 2: The Enhanced Transparency Framework (ETF)

This module focuses on the purpose and scope of the ETF under the Paris Agreement, as well as the challenges and support mechanisms for developing countries in meeting ETF obligations. It also explores the key principles of transparency, accuracy, consistency, comparability, and timeliness that guide ETF reporting and the role of MRV systems in enabling ETF compliance [^1].

### Module 3: Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) Systems

This module introduces MRV systems, focusing on their components: measurement, reporting, and verification. It also covers the IPCC Guidelines for MRV in the AFOLU sector, data collection and reporting requirements, and methods for verifying MRV systems, including internal and external verification and third-party audits [^1].

### Module 4: MRV in Agriculture, Livestock, Forestry, and Other Land Use (AFOLU)

This module explores emissions from agricultural practices, land-use changes, and carbon sequestration potential in land-based activities. It also introduces technologies such as remote sensing, GIS, and carbon accounting tools for improving MRV in the AFOLU sector [^1].

### Module 5: Designing and Implementing an MRV System in Sri Lanka

This module reviews Sri Lanka’s Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) and current MRV frameworks, and outlines steps to design a national MRV system for the AFOLU sector. It also addresses practical considerations for implementing MRV, including data integrity, capacity building, and stakeholder coordination [^1].

### Module 6: Capacity Building and Stakeholder Engagement

This module emphasizes the importance of capacity building for government institutions and local stakeholders to ensure effective MRV systems. It also explores the role of universities, NGOs, and the private sector in supporting MRV capacity building and methods for engaging stakeholders in the MRV process [^1].

### Module 7: Monitoring Progress, Evaluating Effectiveness, and Continuous Improvement

This module covers the development of a database and web portal for reporting and data sharing, the evaluation of MRV system effectiveness using key performance indicators (KPIs), and continuous monitoring and feedback mechanisms to improve the MRV system over time [^1].

### Module 8: Future Directions and Policy Recommendations

This module explores pathways to achieving full ETF compliance and develops policy recommendations for strengthening MRV in Sri Lanka. It also outlines the development of a roadmap for MRV implementation aligned with Sri Lanka’s climate commitments [^1].
