# Strategic Management for Sustainability

Strategic management for sustainability is a theoretical framework that integrates organizational resources and capabilities with the dimensions of sustainability to support the development of sustainable strategies. This approach is grounded in the natural resource-based view (NRBV), which emphasizes the role of natural resources in creating competitive advantage and fostering sustainable value creation. The framework aims to guide organizations in identifying their internal resources and capabilities, while also encouraging the integration of sustainability into strategic decision-making processes. It highlights the importance of aligning organizational activities with environmental, social, and economic goals to achieve long-term value appropriation by consumers and stakeholders.

## Background

The concept of sustainability has evolved significantly over the past few decades, driven by increasing awareness of environmental degradation, social inequality, and economic instability. Early theories of strategic management, such as the resource-based view (RBV), focused primarily on internal organizational capabilities and their role in achieving competitive advantage. However, these theories often overlooked the environmental and social dimensions of sustainability, leading to a gap in understanding how organizations can integrate sustainability into their strategic frameworks.

The natural resource-based view (NRBV) emerged as a response to this gap, emphasizing the importance of natural resources in shaping organizational performance and competitive advantage. This perspective highlights the need for organizations to consider the environmental costs of resource extraction and the broader societal benefits of sustainable practices. The development of the triple bottom line (TBL) framework further expanded the scope of sustainability, advocating for a balanced integration of environmental, social, and economic goals.

## Strategic Management for Sustainability

Strategic management for sustainability is a multidimensional approach that incorporates economic, environmental, and social considerations into organizational decision-making. This framework is designed to be flexible and adaptable, enabling its application across diverse organizational types and contexts. It encourages organizations to consider how their offerings—goods or services—can generate value for the enterprise while fostering value appropriation by customers. This value creation is viewed through three key aspects: a strong value proposition from economic and financial perspectives, the well-being provided to customers, and the alignment with the organization’s sustainable mission.

The framework also emphasizes the importance of considering regional and cultural contexts, as well as the role of knowledge-building in sustainable practices. It calls for future empirical studies to test, replicate, validate, or refine the framework, contributing to its practical relevance and potential evolution. By integrating sustainability into strategic management, organizations can achieve superior results and contribute to the long-term well-being of society and the environment.
