# Strategic Sustainability Communication

Strategic Sustainability Communication is a multidisciplinary field that explores the role of communication in advancing sustainable practices within organizations and society. It encompasses a range of approaches, from the instrumental use of communication to achieve organizational goals to more transformative and co-creative processes that aim to redefine societal norms and values. This field is increasingly recognized as essential for addressing global challenges such as climate change and ecological degradation.

## Background

The concept of strategic sustainability communication has evolved in response to growing awareness of the interconnectedness between business practices and societal well-being. In recent years, there has been a significant shift in academic and practical discourse regarding the relationship between business and society, leading to the development of innovative management concepts such as Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR), Corporate Governance, and Sustainability Management. These concepts have been the subject of extensive research and are now widely discussed in both academic and professional contexts.

The field of strategic sustainability communication is characterized by its interdisciplinary nature, drawing insights from management theory, social sciences, and various cultural perspectives. It is grounded in the idea that communication can play a pivotal role in shaping sustainable practices and fostering a shared understanding of sustainability as a normative principle.

## Strategic Sustainability Communication

Strategic sustainability communication can be understood as the purposeful use of communication by an organization to fulfill its mission and achieve its objectives. From a functionalist perspective, this involves the strategic use of communication to influence audience attitudes, behaviors, and perceptions. This approach is often associated with corporate reporting, marketing, and public relations efforts that incorporate sustainability as a key theme.

However, there is a broader perspective that views strategic sustainability communication as a process of meaning-making and co-creation. This approach emphasizes the role of communication in shaping stakeholder relationships and fostering a shared understanding of sustainability. It includes the management of sustainability-related issues, active participation in related discourses, and the co-creation of forums and spaces for discussing sustainability, such as industry networks, CSR initiatives, and think tanks.

A third perspective on strategic sustainability communication goes beyond the instrumental and co-creative approaches, viewing communication as a transformative process that can stimulate societal change. This perspective emphasizes the role of communication in redefining the "common good" and re-focusing on the issues that are most important for internal and external stakeholders. It also highlights the potential for re-calibrating organizational structures, processes, and goals within a crisis narrative.
