# VSME Digital Template and XBRL Taxonomy

The VSME Digital Template and XBRL Taxonomy are tools developed by the EFRAG Secretariat to support micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with fewer than 250 employees in implementing the Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for non-listed SMEs (VSME). These tools aim to standardize sustainability reporting and reduce the complexity of ESG data requests for SMEs. The document provides an explanatory note on the latest version of these tools, released in June 2026.

## Background

On 17 December 2024, EFRAG issued the Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for non-listed SMEs (VSME). This standard was developed outside the scope of the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) to address the market's need for a common reference point for SMEs to respond to sustainability data requests from business partners. The primary goal is to reduce the burden of multiple ESG data requests by providing a standardized framework for SMEs to report sustainability data.

Following EFRAG’s technical advice on the VSME to the European Commission, EFRAG has been working on the roll-out of the VSME Ecosystem, which includes complementary initiatives to promote market acceptance of the VSME as a standardized tool for SMEs. The ecosystem spans until November 2025, and EFRAG has extended its support for the VSME Ecosystem through 2026. The VSME Digital Template, XBRL Taxonomy, and Excel to XBRL Converter will be maintained at least until the end of 2026.

The VSME Digital Template and XBRL Taxonomy fall within the fifth deliverable "VSME Support Guides" as they were requested by preparers and users during the public consultation of the VSME Exposure Draft from 22 January to 21 May 2024. The EFRAG Secretariat consulted SME Forum members and the Digital Reporting Consultative Forum (DRCF) before releasing the first version in May 2025.

In response to the European Commission's release of the VSME Recommendation on 30 July 2025, the EFRAG Secretariat updated the VSME Digital Template and XBRL Taxonomy to incorporate minor changes from the Recommendation. The updated versions now include labels in 12 languages, including Slovenian, to facilitate usage across all EU member states. Additional user-friendly features, such as language switching and footnotes, have been introduced to enhance the template's usability. The Excel to XBRL converter has also been enhanced with customization options, and the data migration tool has been improved to automatically migrate data from previous versions of the template.

## Objective of the VSME Digital Template and XBRL Taxonomy

The primary purpose of the VSME Digital Template and converter is to illustrate a technical reporting solution and provide educational materials. The VSME Digital Template is not intended to compete with commercial providers, and all materials are provided for free. The template has basic but solid functionality, with a focus on simplicity and user-friendliness. All materials developed by the EFRAG Secretariat are released for free and as open source (MIT license), enabling stakeholders to enhance and integrate them into commercial solutions, provided that EFRAG is referenced appropriately.

The VSME XBRL Taxonomy, maintained by EFRAG, ensures that the data structure and data point definitions remain unchanged and reflect the VSME text. EFRAG intends to provide illustrations of how the XBRL taxonomy and VSME Digital Template can be extended to enable entity or bank-specific additional disclosures.

## Description of the VSME Digital Template

The VSME Digital Template enables user-friendly data entry, including auto-calculation of totals, validations, and consistency checks using formulae and data validation. It also includes drop-downs and checkboxes for semi-narrative disclosures, links to the VSME Text (paragraph level) for each cell or column, and color-coding and tool tips for different categories of data points (e.g., Basic/Comprehensive Module, If Applicable).

## Description of the VSME XBRL Taxonomy

The VSME XBRL Taxonomy enables the digitization of VSME disclosures by providing a tool-independent data model of the VSME disclosures, enabling reporting in various technical formats (Inline XBRL, XBRL-XML, XBRL-JSON, XBRL-CSV). It also facilitates data exchange and import to databases with a structured, license-free, and open format, standardizing the process.
