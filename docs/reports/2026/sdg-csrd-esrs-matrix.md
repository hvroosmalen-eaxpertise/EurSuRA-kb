# SDG–CSRD–ESRS Alignment Matrix

The **SDG–CSRD–ESRS Alignment Matrix** is a mapping framework developed by the [[European Financial Reporting Advisory Group]] (EFRAG) that cross-references the seventeen [[United Nations Sustainable Development Goals]] (SDGs) with the reporting requirements of the [[Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)]] (CSRD) and the corresponding [[European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)]] (ESRS).[^1] The matrix identifies, for each SDG, the relevant ESRS topic, the anticipated corporate impact, recommended actions, and suggested indicators and [[Key Performance Indicators (ESG)]] (KPIs).[^1] The document was designated for internal use only at the time of its drafting.[^1]

---

## Background

The [[Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)]] is a European Union legislative instrument that requires large and listed companies to disclose information on their sustainability impacts, risks, and opportunities.[^1] The [[European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)]], developed by EFRAG, provide the technical standards underpinning CSRD disclosures, covering environmental, social, and governance (ESG) topics across a series of numbered standards.[^1] The [[United Nations Sustainable Development Goals]] constitute a set of seventeen interlinked global goals adopted by all UN member states in 2015 as part of the [[2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development]].[^1] The matrix seeks to operationalise the relationship between these three frameworks, enabling organisations to align their mandatory CSRD/ESRS reporting with voluntary SDG commitments.[^1]

---

## Structure of the Matrix

The matrix is organised into five columns for each of the seventeen SDGs: (1) the relevant CSRD reporting requirement, (2) the corresponding ESRS subject, (3) the anticipated impact on the reporting entity, (4) recommended actions and measures, and (5) proposed indicators and KPIs.[^1] The ESRS standards referenced span the environmental series (ESRS E1–E6), the social series (ESRS S1–S6), and the governance series (ESRS G1–G2).[^1]

---

## SDG Mappings

### SDG 1: No Poverty

The matrix maps SDG 1 to social impact and community engagement requirements under the CSRD, linking this to **ESRS S1** (Own Workforce).[^1] The anticipated corporate impact is improvement of local communities. Recommended actions include investment in local projects and initiatives, with suggested indicators covering the number of projects supported and the number of people assisted.[^1]

### SDG 2: Zero Hunger

SDG 2 is associated with food security and sustainable agriculture, mapped to **ESRS E2** (Pollution).[^1] The anticipated impact relates to improvement of food supply chains through implementation of sustainable agricultural practices. Proposed indicators include the percentage of sustainable agriculture adopted and levels of food waste.[^1]

### SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being

SDG 3 corresponds to occupational health and safety requirements under the CSRD, linked to **ESRS S2** (Health and Safety).[^1] The expected corporate impact is improvement of employee health, achieved through health and well-being programmes. Suggested KPIs include absenteeism rates and the number of health programmes implemented.[^1]

### SDG 4: Quality Education

SDG 4 is associated with education and training initiatives, mapped to **ESRS S3** (Training and Development).[^1] The anticipated impact is enhancement of employee skills and knowledge through training programmes and partnerships with educational institutions. Indicators include the number of training hours and the number of institutional partnerships.[^1]

### SDG 5: Gender Equality

SDG 5 is linked to diversity and inclusion requirements, corresponding to **ESRS S4** (Diversity and Inclusion).[^1] The anticipated impact is promotion of gender equality in the workplace through equal opportunity policies. Proposed KPIs include the percentage of women in leadership positions and the [[gender pay gap]].[^1]

### SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation

SDG 6 is mapped to water management and consumption requirements, corresponding to **ESRS E3** (Water and Marine Resources).[^1] The anticipated corporate impact is reduction of water consumption and pollution through water-saving initiatives and wastewater management. Indicators include water consumption per unit of production and wastewater quality.[^1]

### SDG 7: Affordable and Clean Energy

SDG 7 corresponds to energy consumption and emissions reporting requirements under the CSRD, linked to **ESRS E1** (Climate Change).[^1] The anticipated impact is reduction of energy costs and carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions through investment in renewable energy sources. Suggested KPIs include the percentage of renewable energy used and CO₂ emissions per unit of production.[^1]

### SDG 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth

SDG 8 is associated with working conditions and economic performance, mapped to **ESRS S5** (Working Conditions).[^1] The expected corporate impact is improvement of working conditions and productivity through fair wages and safe working environments. Proposed indicators include employment growth rates and productivity per employee.[^1]

### SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

SDG 9 is linked to innovation and sustainable infrastructure requirements, corresponding to **ESRS E4** (Material Use and Circular Economy).[^1] The anticipated impact is improvement of infrastructure and technological innovation through investment in research and development (R&D) and sustainable infrastructure. Suggested indicators include the number of innovative projects and infrastructure investment levels.[^1]

### SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities

SDG 10 corresponds to equal opportunities and inclusion requirements, linked to **ESRS S6** (Equal Opportunities).[^1] The anticipated corporate impact is reduction of inequalities within and between countries through inclusion and equal opportunity policies. Proposed KPIs include the [[Gini coefficient]] and the number of inclusion initiatives.[^1]

### SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities

SDG 11 is mapped to urban development and community engagement, corresponding to **ESRS E5** (Biodiversity and Ecosystems).[^1] The anticipated impact is improvement of urban liveability and sustainability through sustainable construction practices and community projects. Indicators include the number of sustainable buildings and levels of community engagement.[^1]

### SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

SDG 12 is associated with sustainable resource use requirements, linked to **ESRS E6** (Waste Management).[^1] The anticipated corporate impact is reduction of waste and promotion of recycling through implementation of [[circular economy]] practices. Proposed KPIs include waste reduction rates and recycling percentages.[^1]

### SDG 13: Climate Action

SDG 13 corresponds to climate risk and opportunity reporting requirements under the CSRD, linked to **ESRS E1** (Climate Change).[^1] The anticipated impact is adaptation to climate change through development of a [[climate adaptation]] plan. The suggested indicator is the number of climate adaptation measures implemented.[^1]

### SDG 14: Life Below Water

SDG 14 is mapped to protection of marine ecosystems, corresponding to **ESRS E3** (Water and Marine Resources).[^1] The anticipated corporate impact is reduction of pollution and protection of biodiversity through sustainable fisheries management and establishment of marine protected areas. Indicators include pollution levels and marine biodiversity metrics.[^1]

### SDG 15: Life on Land

SDG 15 is associated with protection of terrestrial ecosystems, linked to **ESRS E5** (Biodiversity and Ecosystems).[^1] The anticipated impact is reduction of deforestation and protection of biodiversity through sustainable forestry management and ecosystem restoration. Proposed KPIs include deforestation rates and terrestrial biodiversity indicators.[^1]

### SDG 16: Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

SDG 16 corresponds to ethical governance and compliance requirements under the CSRD, linked to **ESRS G1** (Governance).[^1] The anticipated corporate impact is improvement of transparency and accountability through implementation of ethical guidelines and compliance programmes. Suggested indicators include the number of compliance incidents and a transparency index.[^1]

### SDG 17: Partnerships for the Goals

SDG 17 is mapped to cooperation and partnerships requirements, corresponding to **ESRS G2** (Cooperation and Partnerships).[^1] The anticipated impact is improvement of stakeholder collaboration through development of partnerships for sustainable development. Proposed KPIs include the number of partnerships established and the number of joint projects undertaken.[^1]
