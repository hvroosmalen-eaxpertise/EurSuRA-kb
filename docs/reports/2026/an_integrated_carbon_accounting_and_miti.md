# Integrated Carbon Accounting and Mitigation (INCAM) Framework

The Integrated Carbon Accounting and Mitigation (INCAM) framework is a systematic approach designed to track carbon emissions and identify potential emission reduction strategies within industrial settings. This framework enables organizations to quantify carbon emissions, monitor carbon emission profiles, and implement effective measures to reduce emissions, thereby contributing to the greening of industry. The INCAM framework is structured to provide relevant information that supports decision-making and planning for sustainable development.

## Background

Greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from industry are a significant contributor to global warming. Within industrial operations, carbon emissions can be categorized into two primary types: direct emissions, which originate from on-site activities such as fuel combustion, and indirect emissions, which result from resource consumption and waste management at external premises. These emissions are influenced by factors such as electricity, fuel consumption, water usage, solid waste, and wastewater. The INCAM framework was developed to address the lack of an integrated approach for quantifying green industry performance, which includes these five main emission contributors.

## Integrated Carbon Accounting and Mitigation (INCAM) Framework

The INCAM framework is designed to serve two primary purposes: tracking emissions within specific on-site areas and identifying potential emission reduction strategies in a holistic manner. The systematic development of the INCAM framework includes the following steps:

1. **Define Carbon Accounting Centre (CAC):** The premise and processes are divided into smaller scoping units for monitoring.
2. **Establish Carbon Emission Indicators (CEI):** For each CAC and carbon performance indicator (CPI), CEI is established using emission factors to calculate carbon emissions from predetermined sources.
3. **Identify Hotspots:** The highest carbon emission profile (%) for each CAC and CPI is determined to identify areas with the highest emissions.
4. **Propose Emission Reduction Strategies:** Options for emission reduction strategies are identified and ranked based on cost-effectiveness.

The INCAM framework provides relevant information that makes carbon profiling visible to various levels of an organization, enabling industry to plan, make decisions, and take effective action to reduce emissions towards greening the industry.

## Case Study: Innovalues Precision Sdn. Bhd.

The effectiveness of the INCAM methodology was demonstrated through a case study at Innovalues Precision Sdn. Bhd., a company located in Pasir Gudang, Johor, Malaysia. This company specializes in the manufacture of customised precision machine parts and components, including printer rollers, mechanical devices, rubber compounding and moulding services, and surface treatment services. The production output for the company is 10,261 t/mth, with products mainly involving surface treatment services such as electroless nickel plating, zinc phosphating, and hard anodizing, used in automotive, office automation equipment, and oil and gas industries.

### Process Description

Nickel electroplating is a process of depositing nickel on a metal part. Parts to be plated must be clean and free of dirt, corrosion, and defects before plating can begin. Water rinsing techniques are used to clean and protect the part during the plating process. The part is immersed into an electrolyte solution and used as the cathode, with the nickel anode dissolving into the electrolyte in the form of nickel ions. These ions travel through the solution and deposit on the cathode. After rinsing to remove residue, the part undergoes a neutralization process, followed by a hot water bathing and drying process. Finally, the parts are sent to the packaging department before being distributed to customers.
