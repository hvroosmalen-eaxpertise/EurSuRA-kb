# Cross-Reference Matrix

This matrix maps the ESRS topical standards to equivalent or related standards in GRI, TCFD, the EU Taxonomy objectives, and the UN SDGs. Use it to identify overlapping disclosure requirements when reporting under multiple frameworks.

| ESRS Topic | ESRS Code | GRI Standard(s) | TCFD Alignment | EU Taxonomy Objective | Primary SDGs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Climate Change | E1 | GRI 302 (Energy), GRI 305 (Emissions) | All 4 pillars (Governance, Strategy, Risk Mgmt, Metrics & Targets) | 1 — Climate change mitigation, 2 — Climate change adaptation | SDG 7, SDG 13 |
| Pollution | E2 | GRI 305 (Air emissions), GRI 306 (Waste) | Physical risk (chronic) | 5 — Pollution prevention and control | SDG 3, SDG 14, SDG 15 |
| Water and Marine Resources | E3 | GRI 303 (Water and Effluents) | Physical risk (water stress) | 3 — Water and marine resources | SDG 6, SDG 14 |
| Biodiversity and Ecosystems | E4 | GRI 304 (Biodiversity) | Physical risk (nature-related) | 6 — Biodiversity and ecosystems | SDG 14, SDG 15 |
| Resource Use and Circular Economy | E5 | GRI 301 (Materials), GRI 306 (Waste) | — | 4 — Circular economy transition | SDG 12 |

## VSME Alignment

| VSME Disclosure | Corresponding ESRS | Corresponding GRI |
|---|---|---|

## Notes on Cross-Reference Methodology

- ESRS and GRI are **interoperable but not identical**: both cover impact materiality, but ESRS adds financial materiality (double materiality) and specific quantitative data points beyond GRI.
- TCFD is **embedded within ESRS E1**: the four TCFD pillars correspond directly to the ESRS E1 disclosure structure.
- EU Taxonomy **objectives** cross-cut the ESRS environmental standards; ESRS E1–E5 each relate to one or more taxonomy objectives, but taxonomy-alignment reporting follows a separate calculation methodology.
- SDG mappings are **indicative**: companies may link additional SDGs based on their sector and materiality assessment.
