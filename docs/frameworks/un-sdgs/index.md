The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), adopted by the United Nations in 2015, comprise 17 goals addressing global challenges including poverty, inequality, climate change, and environmental degradation. The document treats ESG standards as a primary mechanism for enhancing business sector engagement in implementing SDG objectives. The handbook itself was produced in partnership with UNDP Bosnia and Herzegovina and explicitly aims to align business operations with the SDGs. The UNDP SDG Business Pioneers Award in BiH, highlighted through case studies of companies such as Kakanj Cement Plant, Smrčak, ETMAX, and Klika, further illustrates how SDG alignment is recognised and promoted at the national business level.
