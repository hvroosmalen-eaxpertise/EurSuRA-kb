# Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)

The **Global Reporting Initiative (GRI)** is an independent international organisation that has pioneered sustainability reporting since 1997. The GRI Standards are the world's most widely used framework for sustainability reporting, enabling organisations to publicly disclose their economic, environmental, and social impacts.

## GRI Standards Structure

The GRI Standards are organised into three tiers:

### Universal Standards (apply to all organisations)

| Standard | Title | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| GRI 1 | Foundation 2021 | Explains the purpose of the GRI Standards and the key concepts that underpin them |
| GRI 2 | General Disclosures 2021 | Information about an organisation's reporting practices, activities, and governance |
| GRI 3 | Material Topics 2021 | Process for determining material topics and how to manage and report on them |

### Sector Standards

Sector-specific guidance for high-impact industries (oil and gas, coal, agriculture, mining, financial services, etc.), supplementing the topic standards.

### Topic Standards

Topic standards are grouped into three series:

**200 Series — Economic**

## GRI and ESRS Interoperability

EFRAG and GRI published a joint interoperability index mapping the [[ESRS — European Sustainability Reporting Standards|ESRS]] topical standards to GRI topic standards. Companies already reporting under GRI can use this index to identify the additional disclosures required under CSRD. The mapping is not one-to-one: ESRS introduces impact materiality through [[Double Materiality]] assessment and additional quantitative data points not present in GRI.

## Relationship to Other Standards

- **CSRD / ESRS**: GRI and ESRS are complementary. ESRS is mandatory for in-scope EU companies; GRI remains voluntary but is widely used globally.
- **ISSB (IFRS S1, S2)**: GRI covers impact materiality (outward-facing); ISSB focuses on financial materiality (investor-facing). Together they cover double materiality.
- **UN SDGs**: GRI publishes SDG sector guidance and a linkage document mapping GRI disclosures to the 17 goals.
