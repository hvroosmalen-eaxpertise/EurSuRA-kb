The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) is the most widely adopted sustainability reporting framework in the world, first published in 1997. It provides guidance for organisations disclosing how their activities impact people and the planet across economic, environmental, and social themes. The GRI Standards 2021 edition is currently in effect. Adoption of GRI is voluntary, but it is used by organisations across sectors and geographies seeking broad stakeholder accountability. GRI is considered most appropriate for organisations seeking broad impact disclosure and multi-stakeholder accountability, and is designed to be interoperable or complementary with other frameworks.
