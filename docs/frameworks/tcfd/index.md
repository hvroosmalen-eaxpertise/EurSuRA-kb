The Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) was launched in 2017 to help organisations disclose climate-related financial risks in a consistent and decision-useful manner. Its framework is structured around four core pillars: Governance, Strategy, Risk Management, and Metrics and Targets. TCFD disclosures are mandatory for listed companies in several jurisdictions, including the United Kingdom, Japan, and New Zealand. TCFD subsequently served as the foundational basis for the standards developed by the ISSB, and its recommendations also underpin the methodology used by the Carbon Disclosure Project (CDP).
