# Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)

The **Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)**, formally Directive (EU) 2022/2464, is a European Union legislative act that significantly expands the scope and detail of mandatory sustainability reporting for companies operating within the EU. It entered into force on 5 January 2023, replacing the Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD).

## Key Requirements

- Companies must report in accordance with the [[ESRS — European Sustainability Reporting Standards|ESRS]]
- Reports must be included in the management report and subject to limited assurance
- [[Double Materiality]] assessment is mandatory
- Digital tagging (XBRL) is required for machine-readable reporting

## Scope and Phasing

| Phase | Applies from | Companies covered |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | FY 2024 | Large PIEs with >500 employees (previously NFRD in scope) |
| Phase 2 | FY 2025 | Other large companies (>250 employees or €40M turnover or €20M assets) |
| Phase 3 | FY 2026 | Listed SMEs (with opt-out until 2028) |
