# Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for non-listed SMEs (VSME)

The **VSME** is a voluntary sustainability reporting standard developed by the **European Financial Reporting Advisory Group (EFRAG)** specifically for non-listed small and medium enterprises (SMEs). It was published in December 2024.

## Purpose

The VSME addresses the information gap between large companies (in scope of [[CSRD — Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive|CSRD]]) and their SME suppliers, which are frequently asked to provide sustainability data by customers, banks, and investors. The VSME offers a standardised, proportionate format for SMEs to respond to these information requests without incurring the full burden of a CSRD-level disclosure.

## Structure

The VSME has a modular, two-tier structure:

### Module B — Basic

A concise set of general disclosures covering the minimum sustainability information requested in supply chain and financing contexts:

- General information about the company and its sustainability approach
- Business, strategy, and outlook
- Own workforce (headcount, turnover, accidents)
- Environmental practices (energy use, GHG emissions, water, waste — simplified metrics)
- Governance (policies, management responsibility)

### Module BP — Business Partners (optional add-on)

An optional supplementary module for SMEs that wish to respond to more detailed requests from large customers or financial institutions. Covers additional topical disclosures aligned with the structure of the [[ESRS — European Sustainability Reporting Standards|ESRS]] topical standards, scaled down to SME capacity.

## Relationship to ESRS and CSRD

The VSME is not mandatory. Listed SMEs may be subject to a simplified version of CSRD (LSME standards), but non-listed SMEs are not in scope of CSRD. The VSME was designed to be:

- **Interoperable** with CSRD/ESRS: VSME data points can feed directly into the value-chain disclosures that large CSRD reporters must collect
- **Proportionate**: metrics and narrative requirements are calibrated to SME capacity — no double materiality assessment required
- **Credible**: based on the ESRS conceptual framework but simplified

## Relevance to EurSuRA

EurSuRA is a reference architecture for SMEs navigating sustainability reporting obligations. The VSME defines the baseline set of sustainability disclosures that an SME may need to produce, and its data points can be mapped to the EurSuRA data model and the relevant ESRS topical standards.
