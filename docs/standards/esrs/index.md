# European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)

The **European Sustainability Reporting Standards (ESRS)** are a set of mandatory disclosure standards developed by the European Financial Reporting Advisory Group (EFRAG) under the mandate of the [[Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD)]]. They specify the content, structure, and presentation requirements for sustainability information that in-scope companies must include in their management reports.

## Structure

The ESRS are organised into two categories:

- **Cross-cutting standards** (ESRS 1, ESRS 2) — general requirements and general disclosures applicable to all topics
- **Topical standards** — topic-specific requirements across three pillars:
    - **Environmental** (ESRS E1–E5): climate change, pollution, water, biodiversity, circular economy
    - **Social** (ESRS S1–S4): own workforce, value chain workers, affected communities, consumers
    - **Governance** (ESRS G1): business conduct
