# EU Taxonomy Regulation

The **EU Taxonomy Regulation** (Regulation (EU) 2020/852) establishes a classification system for determining whether an economic activity qualifies as environmentally sustainable. It provides companies, investors, and policymakers with a common language for identifying sustainable investments and activities.

## Six Environmental Objectives

An activity is taxonomy-aligned if it contributes substantially to at least one objective and does no significant harm (DNSH) to the others:

1. Climate change mitigation
2. Climate change adaptation
3. Sustainable use and protection of water and marine resources
4. Transition to a circular economy
5. Pollution prevention and control
6. Protection and restoration of biodiversity and ecosystems

## Relationship to CSRD

Companies in scope of the [[CSRD]] must report their taxonomy alignment (eligible and aligned turnover, CapEx, OpEx) as part of their ESRS disclosures.
